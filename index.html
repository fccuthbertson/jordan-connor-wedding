Good to know

A few answers before you ask — and if we missed something, just reach out.

When should I RSVP?

As soon as you can! Use the RSVP button at the top of the page.

Can I stay at the castle?

If you're staying on the property, check-in begins at 3pm on Monday, March 22 and check-out is at 11am on Thursday, March 25.

What should I wear?

Whatever makes you feel your best. March in Scotland is chilly (40s°F) — bring layers!

Do I need a car?

Renting a car is the easiest way to reach the castle, especially from Edinburgh. Buses and trains get you to Laurencekirk, 6–8 miles from the venue.
